I am mainly interested in higher structures in differential geometry, in particular Lie n-groupoids. In my master thesis, I worked on shifted symplectic Lie n-groupoids and Morita equivalences between them. Currently, I am working on transgression and representations of Lie 2-groups with the goal of better understanding geometric quantization for higher structures like the classifying stack BG.
